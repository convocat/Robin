## Ordering and payment

You can order on our website 24 hours a day. Free shipping on all orders from €50!

After ordering, you receive a confirmation email with your order number. Your order number starts with NC- followed by six digits, for example NC-123456.

We accept iDEAL, credit card, PayPal and Klarna (pay within 30 days). Gift cards can be used for all orders.

You can change or cancel your order until it has been shipped. Contact customer service as soon as possible.

## Delivery

We deliver in the Netherlands and Belgium.

| Country | Delivery time |
|---|---|
| Netherlands | 1 to 3 working days |
| Belgium | 2 to 4 working days |

Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are usually shipped the same day.

Express delivery is available in the Netherlands for a small fee. With express delivery, your order arrives the next working day. Express delivery is not available for large items.

Delivery is free for orders above €75. Below that amount, delivery costs €5.95.

Large items, such as sofas, cabinets and rugs over 2 metres, are delivered by our own courier. The courier delivers to the front door and contacts you to plan a delivery day.

**Is your order late?** Please wait 2 business days after the expected delivery date before contacting us. In most cases, the parcel is still on its way. Most orders arrive within a week.

## Returns

Return policy:

- Customers can return products within 30 days of delivery.
- Products must be unused and in their original packaging.
- Made-to-measure products can't be returned.
- Returns cost €4.95, deducted from the refund. Returning a damaged product is free.
- The refund is paid within 14 days after the return arrives.

To return a product, request a return label from Customer Care. Pack the product, stick the label on the box and drop it off at a parcel point.

**Return costs per country**

| Country | Return costs | Carrier |
|---|---|---|
| Netherlands | €4.95 | PostNL |
| Belgium | €6.95 | bpost |
| Germany | €7.95 | DHL Parcel |

Large items are collected by our courier. Customer Care will plan a pickup date with you.

## Damaged or faulty products

Check your order when it arrives. If a product is damaged, take a photo and contact customer service within 48 hours of delivery. You can call us 7 days a week.

If a product breaks during normal use, you can rely on the legal guarantee. Contact Customer Care with your order number and a photo of the problem. We will offer a repair, a replacement or a refund. In some cases, Customer Care may offer a voucher instead.

## Late deliveries in peak season

In November and December, customers whose order arrives more than 5 working days late receive a €10 voucher.

*Note for agents: only offer the voucher if the customer asks about compensation. Don't mention it proactively.*

## Sale and outlet

Products in the sale have a red price tag on the website. Sale items can be exchanged for another product, but are not refunded.

Outlet products may have small cosmetic flaws, which are described on the product page. Outlet products can't be returned or exchanged.

## Product care

**Wooden furniture.** Dust with a dry, soft cloth. Avoid direct sunlight and place furniture at least 30 cm from radiators. Treat the wood with oil once or twice a year. See the care guide (CS-CG-002) for the right products.

**Sofas and upholstery.** Vacuum the upholstery regularly with a soft brush. Blot spills straight away with a clean, damp cloth; don't rub. Small colour differences and light pilling on fabric are normal and are not a defect.

**Rugs.** Vacuum once a week, without a rotating brush for wool rugs. Turn the rug every few months so it wears evenly. Professional cleaning is recommended once a year.

**Ceramics and glass.** Most of our vases and tableware are dishwasher safe. Handmade items should be washed by hand. Check the product page.

**Lamps.** Use a bulb with the maximum wattage stated on the lamp. Clean with a dry cloth, never while the lamp is switched on.

## Styling tips

**Start with one statement piece.** A bold sofa, a large rug or a striking lamp gives a room its character. Keep the rest calm around it.

**Mix materials.** Combine wood, metal and textiles for a warm, layered look. A wooden side table next to a velvet sofa works well.

**Work with three colours.** Choose one main colour, one supporting colour and one accent. Green sofas go well with warm neutrals such as sand and terracotta, with brass as an accent.

**Light in layers.** Combine a ceiling light, a floor lamp and a small table lamp. Warm white light (2700K) makes a living room cosy.

**Leave space.** Keep at least 80 cm walking space around furniture, and don't push every piece against the wall.
