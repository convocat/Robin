## General information

Every product page on our website shows the dimensions, materials, colours and price of the product. This guide collects that information in one place, with care advice per product.

All Nest & Co products come with a 30-day return guarantee and a 2-year guarantee on manufacturing faults.

Prices include VAT. Free delivery from €50. Express delivery costs €9.95 and is available for small items.

Products marked **large item** are delivered by our own courier, to the front door. Products marked **made to measure** are produced after you order.

Article numbers have four digits and three letters, for example 4001-OSL. You'll find the article number on the product page and on the packing slip.
