# Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer here? Chat with Robin, our chatbot, or contact Customer Care on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00.

## Ordering and payment

### How do I place an order?

You can order on our website 24 hours a day. After ordering, you receive a confirmation email with your order number, for example NC-123456.

[Ordering and payment in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#ordering-and-payment)

### How can I pay?

With iDEAL, credit card, PayPal or Klarna (pay within 30 days). You can also use a gift card.

[Ordering and payment in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#ordering-and-payment)

### Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, until your order has been shipped. Contact Customer Care as soon as possible.

[Ordering and payment in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#ordering-and-payment)

## Delivery

### Where do you deliver?

In the Netherlands and Belgium.

[Delivery in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#delivery)

### How long does delivery take?

1 to 3 working days in the Netherlands, and 2 to 4 working days in Belgium. Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are usually shipped the same day.

[Delivery in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#delivery)

### What does delivery cost?

Delivery is free for orders above €75. Below that amount, delivery costs €5.95.

[Delivery in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#delivery)

### Can I get my order faster?

Yes, with express delivery your order arrives the next working day. Express delivery is available in the Netherlands, but not for large items.

[Delivery in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#delivery)

### How are sofas and other large items delivered?

Large items, such as sofas, cabinets and rugs over 2 metres, are delivered by our own courier, to the front door. The courier contacts you to plan a delivery day.

[Delivery in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#delivery)

### My order is late. What should I do?

Please wait 2 working days after the expected delivery date. In most cases, your parcel is still on its way. Still nothing? Contact Customer Care.

[Delivery in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#delivery)

## Returns

### Can I return a product?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

[Returns in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#returns)

### What does a return cost?

A return costs €4.95, which we deduct from your refund. Returning a damaged product is free.

[Returns in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#returns)

### How do I return a product?

Request a return label from Customer Care. Pack the product, stick the label on the box, and drop it off at a parcel point. Large items are collected by our courier: Customer Care plans a pickup date with you.

[Returns in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#returns)

### When do I get my money back?

Within 14 days after your return arrives at our warehouse.

[Returns in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#returns)

### Can I return made-to-measure products?

No. Made-to-measure products, such as curtains or a dining table in your own size, can't be returned.

[Returns in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#returns)

### Can I return a sale or outlet product?

Sale products can be exchanged for another product, but not refunded. Outlet products can't be returned or exchanged.

[Sale and outlet in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#sale-and-outlet)

## Damaged or faulty products

### My order arrived damaged. What now?

Take a photo and contact Customer Care within 48 hours of delivery. Returning a damaged product is free.

[Damaged or faulty products in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#damaged-or-faulty-products)

### A product broke during normal use. What can I do?

You can rely on the legal guarantee. Contact Customer Care with your order number and a photo of the problem. We'll offer a repair, a replacement or a refund.

[Damaged or faulty products in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#damaged-or-faulty-products)

## Gift cards

### How long is a gift card valid?

Gift cards are valid for 2 years. They're available from €10 to €250, and can't be exchanged for cash.

[Gift cards in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#gift-cards)

## Product care

### How do I take care of wooden furniture?

Dust with a dry, soft cloth, avoid direct sunlight, and keep furniture at least 30 cm away from radiators. Treat the wood with oil once or twice a year.

[Product care in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#product-care)

### My sofa fabric is pilling. Is that a defect?

No. Small colour differences and light pilling on fabric are normal, and not a defect.

[Product care in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#product-care)

### Can my vase go in the dishwasher?

Most of our vases and tableware are dishwasher safe. Handmade items should be washed by hand. Check the product page.

[Product care in the customer handbook](/help/handbook#product-care)

By chat and email, on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00. You can expect an answer to emails within 2 working days.
